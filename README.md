### Hi! I'm Sammy.

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at the Space Telescope Science Institute. My research focuses on directly imaging and characterizing exoplanets, with an emphasis on reflected-light observations and high-contrast imaging. I develop models, create computational tools, and analyze observational data, to understand exoplanet observations for observations from the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope and the Habitable Worlds Observatory. I'm also a member of the Roman Coronagraph Community Participation Program, where I support observation planning and tool development.
